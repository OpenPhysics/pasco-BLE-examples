PASCO BLE Examples

Browser demos for the pasco-ble npm package

Requirements

  • A Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera) - Web Bluetooth is not supported in Firefox or Safari
  • A PASCO Wireless BLE sensor or controller
  • Bluetooth enabled on your device
